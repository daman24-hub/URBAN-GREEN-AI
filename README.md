# 🌳 Urban Green AI

> **Plant the right tree, in the right place, at the right time.**

Urban Green AI is an AI-powered urban planning platform that helps cities make smarter and more data-driven urban greening decisions.

The platform combines **AI, GIS, satellite/environmental data, and spatial analysis** to identify suitable locations for tree plantation and recommend appropriate tree species based on local environmental conditions.

## 🚀 Problem

Rapid urbanization has increased:

* 🌡️ Urban heat
* 🏙️ Built-up density
* 💧 Stormwater and flooding risks
* 🌫️ Air pollution
* 🌳 Loss of urban greenery

Traditional tree plantation planning often relies on generalized decisions rather than analyzing the specific environmental conditions of each location.

## 💡 Solution

**Urban Green AI** analyzes multiple urban and environmental factors to recommend:

* 📍 Suitable locations for tree plantation
* 🌱 Appropriate tree species
* 🌡️ Potential urban heat reduction
* 🌧️ Areas where greenery can support stormwater management
* 🌿 Opportunities to improve urban green coverage

## 🧠 Key Factors

The platform can consider factors such as:

| Factor                       | Purpose                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 🌡️ Land Surface Temperature | Identify urban heat hotspots                |
| 🌱 Soil Type                 | Determine suitable vegetation               |
| 🌧️ Rainfall                 | Understand water availability               |
| 🛣️ Road Network             | Identify roadside greening opportunities    |
| 🏢 Building Density          | Detect highly built-up areas                |
| 🌳 Open Spaces               | Find potential plantation areas             |
| 🛰️ Satellite Data           | Understand spatial/environmental conditions |
